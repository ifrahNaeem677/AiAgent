## Delivery
- We ship to the UK, EU and USA.
- Orders placed before 2pm (UK time) Monday-Friday are dispatched the same day.
- UK Standard delivery: 2-4 working days, £3.99, free on orders over £40.
- UK Express delivery: next working day if ordered before 2pm, £6.99.
- EU delivery: 4-7 working days, £9.99.
- USA delivery: 5-10 working days, £14.99. Customers may have to pay import duties.
- Tracking links are emailed as soon as the parcel is dispatched.
- If a parcel shows as delivered but has not arrived, ask the customer to check with neighbours and their safe place, wait 24 hours, then hand off to staff.

## Returns and refunds
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- To start a return the customer visits example.com/returns and enters their order number and email.
- UK returns are free using the prepaid label. International customers pay return postage.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of us receiving the item.
- Sale items can be returned for store credit only.
- Personalised/engraved items cannot be returned unless faulty.
- Faulty or damaged items: we replace or refund in full. Staff must handle these (photos are needed), so hand off to staff.

## Order changes and cancellations
- Orders can be changed or cancelled only before dispatch. The agent cannot do this itself: hand off to staff immediately and mention that time matters.

## Payment
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- Klarna pay-in-3 is available on orders over £30.
- Discount codes are entered at checkout. Only one code per order.

## Contact
- Staff hours: Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm UK time.
- Email: support@example.com
